## Long description (4,000 characters max)

Vexmarch is a real-time grand strategy game. Thirty nodes, four to six factions, and one march to decide who rules them all.

Run your realm from the strategy map. Tax your cities, pick a governor, put up buildings, recruit troops and form armies. Click an army, then a node, to march. Supply lines decide wars, so keep your roads open and your garrisons fed.

Officers run your cities and their loyalty matters. Give them orders, watch them grumble, and promote the ones who earn it. Diplomacy lets you form alliances, exchange hostages, arrange marriages, ask allies for help, or break a pact when it suits you. Every rival is an AI with its own temper.

Walk through your own city, talk to officers and townsfolk, and give gifts while the clock is paused. Watch the 3D city grow from a hamlet to a capital, with walls, towers and a keep. When armies meet, watch the battle unfold in the field view, with soldiers of four kinds, arrow volleys and sieges.

Play a random Skirmish, or the Campaign with its own story, houses, officers, chronicle and several endings. Turn on fog of war or random events for a harder game.

Features:
• Real-time strategy on a 3D map with terrain
• Cities, taxes, buildings, growth and trade routes
• Officers with loyalty, governors and orders
• War with armies, sieges, battles and supply lines
• Diplomacy: alliances, marriages, hostages, betrayal
• 3D city view and Walk mode
• Campaign story, Skirmish mode and three AI levels (Easy, Normal, Hard)
• English and Korean
• Sound effects

Tutorial included. Press Tutorial on the title screen for a guided first game.
